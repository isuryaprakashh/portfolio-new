# Surya Prakash — Portfolio Identity & Direction

## Core Portfolio Identity

The portfolio should feel like a cinematic digital identity rather than a traditional resume website.

It should represent:

* curiosity
* exploration
* modern engineering
* systems thinking
* immersive digital experiences
* collaboration
* continuous learning through building

The overall feeling should be:

* minimal
* immersive
* cinematic
* modern
* intentional
* smooth
* atmospheric
* clean
* slightly artistic
* technically grounded

The website should not feel:

* corporate
* overly emotional
* startup-marketing heavy
* flashy
* overloaded
* gimmicky
* cyberpunk/futuristic
* resume-like

The portfolio should feel:

> calm, confident, modern, and exploratory.

---

# Visual Philosophy

## Design Style

* Cinematic minimalism
* Spacious layouts
* Strong typography hierarchy
* Atmospheric pacing
* Smooth motion
* Quiet confidence

## Motion Style

* Slow fade-ins
* Blur-to-focus reveals
* Smooth scrolling
* Gentle upward motion
* Cinematic transitions between sections
* Subtle parallax
* Slow image reveals while scrolling

Avoid:

* aggressive animations
* bouncing effects
* excessive particles
* heavy 3D scenes
* flashy mouse interactions

## Atmosphere

The cinematic feeling should come from:

* typography
* spacing
* pacing
* visual rhythm
* transitions
* section contrast

NOT from:

* overuse of effects
* heavy graphics
* shader-heavy visuals

---

# Performance Philosophy

The website should remain lightweight and smooth.

Avoid:

* heavy Three.js scenes
* unnecessary WebGL
* performance-heavy animations

Use:

* Framer Motion
* Lenis smooth scrolling
* subtle motion only

The cinematic feel should come from:

* timing
* pacing
* layout
* typography
